# Module 1: Introduction to Physical AI

## Module Overview

Welcome to the first module of the Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics curriculum. This module establishes the foundational concepts that underpin the entire field of embodied artificial intelligence.

Physical AI represents a paradigm shift from traditional AI systems that operate purely in digital space. Instead, Physical AI systems must sense, reason about, and act upon the real physical world through robotic embodiment. This introduces unique challenges and opportunities that we'll explore throughout this module.

### What Makes Physical AI Different?

Traditional AI excels at tasks like:
- Playing chess or Go
- Translating languages
- Generating text or images
- Analyzing data patterns

Physical AI must additionally:
- Navigate uncertain, dynamic environments
- Manipulate objects with varying properties
- Recover from unexpected physical interactions
- Learn from sparse, embodied experiences
- Operate under real-time constraints

This module sets the stage for understanding why embodiment fundamentally changes the nature of intelligence and learning.
