# Module 2: Foundations of Embodied Intelligence

## Module Overview

Building on Module 1's introduction to Physical AI, this module dives deep into the theoretical foundations that make embodied intelligence unique. We explore embodied cognition—the idea that intelligence emerges from the interaction between mind, body, and environment—and examine how this principle transforms AI system design.

This module bridges philosophy, cognitive science, and robotics to provide the conceptual framework for understanding why physical interaction matters for learning and intelligence.

---

## Learning Outcomes

By completing this module, you will be able to:

### 1. Describe the Principles of Embodied Cognition

**What you'll master**:
- Understand the embodied cognition hypothesis from cognitive science
- Explain how cognition is grounded in sensorimotor experience
- Distinguish embodied approaches from classical symbolic AI
- Apply embodied cognition principles to AI system design

**Why it matters**: Embodied cognition provides the theoretical foundation for Physical AI, influencing everything from robot design to learning algorithms.

---

### 2. Explain Sensorimotor Integration Concepts

**What you'll master**:
- Understand how robots integrate perception and action
- Describe the perception-action loop in embodied systems
- Explain forward and inverse models in motor control
- Recognize the role of proprioception and haptic feedback

**Why it matters**: Sensorimotor integration is how robots "make sense" of their physical interactions—critical for manipulation, navigation, and learning.

---

### 3. Understand the Role of Physical Interaction in Learning

**What you'll master**:
- Explain how physical interaction provides learning signal
- Describe developmental robotics and learning by doing
- Understand affordance learning and physical exploration
- Recognize how embodiment constrains and enables learning

**Why it matters**: Physical interaction isn't just execution—it's a unique source of information that shapes how robots learn.

---

## Module Structure

### 1. Embodied Cognition
Explore the theoretical foundations: how cognition emerges from physical interaction, and why intelligence requires bodies.

### 2. Sensorimotor Integration
Learn how robots couple perception and action, creating closed-loop systems that respond to physical feedback.

### 3. Physical Interaction Learning
Discover how robots learn from doing—using physical experience as training signal for improving behavior.

## How This Module Connects

## Key Questions Answered

- What is the perception-action loop, and why does it matter?
- How do robots learn from physical interaction rather than just data?
