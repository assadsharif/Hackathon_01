# Module 4: Perception for Physical AI

## Module Overview

Perception is how robots sense and interpret their physical environment. Unlike traditional computer vision operating on static images, Physical AI perception must handle 3D space, dynamic scenes, and multimodal sensor fusion.

This module explores how robots integrate vision, touch, proprioception, and other modalities to build rich representations of their surroundings—a prerequisite for intelligent action.

---

## Learning Outcomes

By completing this module, you will be able to:

### 1. Explain Multimodal Sensing in Robotics

**What you'll master**:
- Understand the role of vision, touch, proprioception, and force sensing
- Explain sensor fusion and complementary modalities
- Describe how different modalities provide different information
- Recognize when to use which sensing modality

**Why it matters**: Effective perception requires combining multiple sensor types—no single modality provides complete information.

---

### 2. Understand Spatial Awareness and Mapping Concepts

**What you'll master**:
- Explain how robots build 3D representations of space
- Describe SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping) concepts
- Understand depth perception and 3D reconstruction
- Recognize the role of egocentric vs. allocentric representations

**Why it matters**: Spatial awareness enables navigation, manipulation, and planning in 3D environments.

---

### 3. Describe Object Recognition in Physical Contexts

**What you'll master**:
- Understand 3D object recognition vs. 2D image classification
- Explain pose estimation and 6-DOF object detection
- Describe the role of physics in perception (affordances)
- Recognize challenges unique to physical object recognition

**Why it matters**: Recognizing objects is necessary but not sufficient—robots must also understand how to interact with them.

---

### 2. Spatial Awareness
