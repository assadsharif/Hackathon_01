# Module 5: Action and Control Fundamentals

## Module Overview

After perceiving the environment (Module 4), Physical AI systems must act upon it. This module explores how robots plan and execute physical actions—from high-level task planning to low-level motor control.

We examine the control hierarchies that bridge intentions and motor commands, and complete the action-perception loop that defines embodied intelligence.

---

## Learning Outcomes

By completing this module, you will be able to:

### 1. Understand Motion Planning at a Conceptual Level

**What you'll master**:
- Explain path planning and trajectory generation
- Understand configuration space and obstacles
- Describe sampling-based vs. optimization-based planning
- Recognize when to use reactive vs. deliberative control

**Why it matters**: Motion planning enables robots to achieve goals while respecting physical constraints and avoiding collisions.

---

### 2. Describe Control Hierarchies in Humanoid Systems

**What you'll master**:
- Understand layered control architectures (task, motion, joint levels)
- Explain the role of feedback at each control layer
- Describe whole-body control for humanoids
- Recognize how priorities are managed in hierarchical control

**Why it matters**: Complex robots like humanoids require coordinated control across multiple abstraction levels.

---

### 3. Explain the Action-Perception Loop

**What you'll master**:
- Understand how action and perception form a closed loop
- Describe the role of feedback in adaptive behavior
- Explain reactive vs. model-based control
- Recognize how embodiment shapes the control problem

**Why it matters**: The action-perception loop is the fundamental cycle of embodied intelligence—perception informs action, which changes perception.

---

## Module Structure

### 1. Motion Planning
Learn how robots plan collision-free paths and trajectories to achieve goals.

### 2. Control Hierarchies
Explore how control is organized across task, motion, and joint levels.

### 3. Action-Perception Loop
Understand the feedback cycle that enables adaptive, intelligent behavior.
