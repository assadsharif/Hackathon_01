# Module 6: Learning in Physical Environments

## Module Overview

Learning distinguishes AI from traditional robotics. This module explores how Physical AI systems improve through experience—both in simulation and the real world.

We examine the challenges of learning from physical interaction, the simulation-to-reality gap, and how reinforcement learning enables robots to discover effective behaviors through trial and error.

---

## Learning Outcomes

By completing this module, you will be able to:

### 1. Differentiate Simulation-Based vs. Real-World Learning

**What you'll master**:
- Understand the role of simulation in robot learning
- Explain the sim-to-real gap and why it matters
- Describe techniques for bridging simulation and reality
- Recognize when to use simulation vs. real-world training

**Why it matters**: Simulation enables safe, fast, scalable learning—but only if we can transfer what's learned to reality.

---

### 2. Understand Transfer Learning Challenges for Robotics

**What you'll master**:
- Explain domain adaptation for physical systems
- Describe domain randomization and sim-to-real techniques
- Understand the role of physics modeling in transfer
- Recognize what transfers easily vs. what doesn't

**Why it matters**: Transfer learning multiplies the value of simulation by enabling knowledge reuse across domains and platforms.

---

### 3. Describe Reinforcement Learning Concepts for Physical Agents

**What you'll master**:
- Understand RL fundamentals (rewards, policies, value functions)
- Explain how RL applies to physical systems
- Describe sample efficiency challenges in real-world RL
- Recognize when RL is appropriate for Physical AI

**Why it matters**: RL enables robots to learn complex behaviors that are difficult to program manually.

---
